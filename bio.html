BIO

About Me

01.-

Introduction


I am a designer, strategist, researcher, leader, mentor, and storyteller dedicated to fostering innovation and delivering human-centered solutions that drive business success.


My work is grounded in research, systems thinking, and close collaboration with cross-functional teams to transform complexity into clear, scalable experiences. Whether I'm defining information architecture, facilitating workshops, building design systems, or crafting polished interfaces, I approach every project with the same goal: creating products that are intuitive for users, efficient for teams, and aligned with business outcomes.

02.-

Approach

Strategy

I align user needs with business goals before pixels are designed.

Information Architecture

I simplify complex systems into intuitive experiences.

Research

I validate assumptions through quantitative & qualitative research and rapid testing.

Design Systems

I build scalable foundations that help teams move faster.

Leadership

I mentor designers while remaining deeply involved in execution.

AI Accelerated Design

I use AI to speed discovery, synthesis, ideation, documentation, and prototyping, not replace design thinking.

Work History


SERVPRO

2024 - 2026

UX Design Lead

Recruited to build SERVPRO's UX department and practice from the ground up as an individual contributor, establishing the foundational systems and standards that will scale into a full team.

  • Architecting the company's design system and component library in Figma, establishing patterns, standards, and documentation that align marketing, product, and engineering on a shared design language.
  • Leading information architecture, UX research, and UI design end-to-end across the consumer web experience for a national franchise network.
  • Using Userlytics to write test scripts, create prototypes, and synthesize actionable research findings that drive measurable improvements in call volume and franchise revenue.
  • Defining and managing a UX roadmap through sprint planning in Jira, aligning stakeholders across marketing, product, and brand leadership on priorities and outcomes.
  • Championing UX principles with internal and external partners, creating a shared vision for experience standards across the organization.

Tools: Figma, Mural, Adobe CC, Userlytics, Jira, Claude, Replit


Neighborly

2021 - 2024

UX Design Lead

Led UX design strategy and team for a portfolio of 20+ home service franchise brands under one of North America's largest franchise parent companies.

  • Defined a jobs-to-be-done framework and customer personas, grounded in Neighborly's actual customer segmentation, that became the shared decision-making model across 20+ brand stakeholders, from initial workshops through final shipped product.
  • Facilitated cross-brand alignment workshops with brand presidents, PMOs, engineers, marketing, SEO, and CRO stakeholders, converting competing brand priorities into a single shared journey model and phased migration roadmap.
  • Served as UX Lead on Project OPUS, a SaaS CMS platform built on CrownPeak, Tailwind CSS, and Flowbite, migrating 20+ brand websites onto a unified, scalable platform and eliminating fragmented systems across the portfolio.
  • Extended the OPUS information architecture and JTBD framework to the Neighborly native app, shipping it as the app's primary navigation structure (Repair / Maintain / Enhance) and core booking flow.
  • Built and maintained a comprehensive Figma design system and component library shared across all brands, balancing consistency with per-brand flexibility.
  • Defined and led UX research methodology, from planning and scripting to synthesis, presenting findings directly to executive leadership.
  • Managed a blended team of 10, 4 in-house UX designers, 2 UX researchers, 1 ADA specialist, and 3 offshore designers from a contracted third-party agency, and oversaw design sprint cadence across the platform lifecycle.

Tools: Figma, Mural, Axure, Adobe CC, CrownPeak, Azure DevOps, SharePoint
